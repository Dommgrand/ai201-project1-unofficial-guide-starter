RETAILER: Costco

OVERVIEW: Costco is a membership-based warehouse retailer that sells groceries and other products through physical warehouses and online services.

STORE LOCATIONS: Costco’s warehouse locator allows users to search for Costco locations and find information about warehouses in their area.

GROCERY SERVICES: Costco provides grocery shopping through its warehouses and online services such as:

* grocery shopping
* online grocery ordering
* delivery
* warehouse shopping
* same-day grocery services where applicable

CURRENT PRICES AND INVENTORY: This knowledge base does not provide real-time product prices, inventory levels, promotions, or store-specific product availability. These would require a live data source or API.

PICKUP: The sources used for this knowledge base document Costco’s same-day grocery delivery service but do not establish a general grocery pickup service.

DELIVERY: Costco offers same-day grocery delivery in eligible areas.

MEMBERSHIP: Costco’s shopping model is membership-based. Costco offers different membership levels, and membership is associated with purchasing merchandise and services from Costco.
