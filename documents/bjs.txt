RETAILER: BJ's Wholesale Club

OVERVIEW: BJ's Wholesale Club is a membership-based warehouse retailer that sells groceries and other products through physical warehouses and online services.

STORE LOCATIONS: BJ's Wholesale Club's warehouse locator allows users to search for BJ's locations and find information about warehouses in their area.

GROCERY SERVICES: BJ's provides grocery shopping through its physical clubs and online services, including pickup and delivery options for eligible orders.

CURRENT PRICES AND INVENTORY: This knowledge base does not provide real-time product prices, inventory levels, promotions, or store-specific product availability. These would require a live data source or API.

PICKUP: BJ’s offers pickup for eligible orders at participating clubs.

DELIVERY: BJ’s offers same-day delivery. Delivery availability depends on location/service eligibility. Customers can order eligible products online. Delivery is fulfilled to the customer’s address.

MEMBERSHIP: BJ's shopping model is membership-based. BJ's offers different membership options, and membership is associated with purchasing merchandise and services from BJ's.
