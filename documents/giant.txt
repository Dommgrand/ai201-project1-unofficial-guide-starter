RETAILER: Giant Food

OVERVIEW: Giant Food is a grocery retailer that operates stores where customers can shop for groceries in person. Giant Food also provides online grocery ordering and grocery pickup and delivery.

STORE LOCATIONS: Giant Food’s Store Locator allows customers to find Giant Food locations and view available store information and services. Giant provides a Maryland store directory that can be used to find locations throughout the state.

GROCERY SERVICES: Giant Food provides grocery shopping through its physical stores and online ordering. Customers can use available pickup and delivery services to receive groceries, depending on location and service eligibility.

CURRENT PRICES AND INVENTORY: This knowledge base does not provide real-time product prices, inventory levels, promotions, or store-specific product availability. These would require a live data source or API.

PICKUP: Giant Food offers online grocery ordering with pickup available at participating locations.

DELIVERY: Giant Food offers grocery delivery through online grocery ordering. Delivery is provided to the customer’s address, and availability may depend on location and service eligibility.

MEMBERSHIP: Giant Food is not a membership warehouse club, and a membership is not required for customers to shop at its stores.
