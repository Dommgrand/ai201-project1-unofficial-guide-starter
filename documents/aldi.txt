RETAILER: ALDI

OVERVIEW: ALDI is a grocery retailer that operates physical stores where customers primarily shop for groceries. ALDI also provides product and store information through its website.

STORE LOCATIONS: ALDI Store Locator allows users to locate ALDI’s stores.

GROCERY SERVICES: ALDI provides grocery shopping through its physical stores and online services. Customers can use grocery pickup and delivery services where available.

CURRENT PRICES AND INVENTORY: This knowledge base does not provide real-time product prices, inventory levels, promotions, or store-specific product availability. These would require a live data source or API.

PICKUP: ALDI offers grocery pickup for online grocery orders at participating locations. Availability may depend on the customer’s location.

DELIVERY: ALDI offers grocery delivery for online grocery orders in eligible areas. Delivery availability depends on location and service eligibility.

MEMBERSHIP: ALDI does not require a membership to shop at its stores.
