RETAILER: Walmart

OVERVIEW: Walmart is a large retailer that offers grocery shopping through physical stores and Walmart.com/the Walmart app. Customers can purchase groceries for pickup, delivery, or shipping, depending on the items and services available at their location.

STORE LOCATIONS: Walmart’s Store Finder allows users to search by ZIP code or city/state, find nearby stores, view store hours and services, and select a preferred store. The Store Finder also allows filtering by services such as Grocery, Bakery, Deli, Pharmacy, Gas Station, and others.

GROCERY SERVICES: Walmart’s official site says customers can shop for groceries through Walmart.com or the Walmart app and select how they want to receive their items. Available fulfillment methods include:

* Pickup
* Delivery
* Shipping

Walmart also allows users to search for items and filter results based on how and when they want to receive them.

CURRENT PRICES AND INVENTORY: This knowledge base does not provide real-time product prices, inventory levels, promotions, or store-specific product availability. These would require a live data source or API.

PICKUP: Walmart describes pickup as an online ordering process where the customer selects a store and time-slot. Once the order is ready, the customer checks in and goes to a designated curbside pickup space, where a Walmart associate brings out the order. Pickup availability depends on the selected store and order.

DELIVERY: Walmart says customers can enter their address to determine whether delivery is available. If delivery is available, the customer selects a delivery time-slot and the store gathers the order. A local third-party driver may then deliver it to the customer’s door. Delivery availability is location-dependent.

MEMBERSHIP: Walmart membership is optional for ordinary grocery shopping. Walmart+ provides additional benefits, including delivery-related benefits for eligible orders.
