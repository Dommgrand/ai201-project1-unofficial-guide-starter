RETAILER: Target

OVERVIEW: Target is a general merchandise retailer, not a grocery-only retailer. However, groceries are one of the categories customers can purchase through Target’s stores and online services.

STORE LOCATIONS: Target's Store Locator allows customers to find Target locations and view available store information and services. 

GROCERY SERVICES: Target provides grocery shopping through its physical stores and online ordering. Customers can use available pickup and delivery services to receive groceries, depending on location and service eligibility.

CURRENT PRICES AND INVENTORY: This knowledge base does not provide real-time product prices, inventory levels, promotions, or store-specific product availability. These would require a live data source or API.

PICKUP: Target offers online grocery ordering with pickup available at participating locations.

DELIVERY: Target offers grocery delivery through online grocery ordering. Delivery is provided to the customer’s address, and availability may depend on location and service eligibility.


MEMBERSHIP: Target is not a membership warehouse club, and a membership is not required for customers to shop at its stores.
