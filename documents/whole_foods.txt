RETAILER: Whole Foods Market

OVERVIEW: Whole Foods Market is a grocery retailer that operates physical stores where customers primarily shop for groceries. Whole Foods Market also provides product and store information through its website.

STORE LOCATIONS: Whole Foods Market's Store Locator allows users to locate Whole Foods Market’s stores by location.

GROCERY SERVICES: Whole Foods Market provides grocery shopping through its physical stores and online ordering. Customers can use online services to order groceries and access available fulfillment options.

CURRENT PRICES AND INVENTORY: This knowledge base does not provide real-time product prices, inventory levels, promotions, or store-specific product availability. These would require a live data source or API.

PICKUP: Whole Foods Market offers grocery pickup for eligible online grocery orders. Availability depends on location and service eligibility.

DELIVERY: Whole Foods Market offers grocery delivery for eligible online grocery orders. Delivery availability depends on location and service eligibility.

MEMBERSHIP: Whole Foods Market does not require a membership to shop at its stores.
